The Eisenhower Matrix is a productivity and time-management framework that helps you prioritize tasks by categorizing them into four quadrants based on their urgency and importance. It ensures you focus on what truly matters rather than just reacting to time-sensitive distractions.
I have a trouble prioritizing my task. I usually use google keep. My goal here is to create my own version to improve my personal productivity.

🏷️ Tags: Work/Personal/Health/Finance/Study/Project
📝 Notes: Per-task notes with preview on card
✅ CompletionStrikethrough → auto-archives after 5s
🗂️ Archive: View, restore, or clear archived tasks
🔍 Search: Live search across task text + notes
🎛️ Filter: By tag, overdue, or completed
